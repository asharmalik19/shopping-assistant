Shopping Assistant

An in-store helper for people who don't think about clothes. Photograph an item and it tells you, in plain language, whether to buy it, skip it, or look for a variation.

The kernel: what makes this different

It gives a clear, personal verdict because it already knows your body, skin tone, height, style, and what you're shopping for today.

Skip itToo far off; leave it on the rack.
CloseGood item, wrong detail: "look for it in navy or olive."
Buy itFits you, your style, and the occasion.

No jargon. When it does use a term (a color name, a collar style), it shows you what that means instead of just naming it.

The core loop

  1. Once: set up a profile with faceless body photos, height, and style ("minimal but stylish, not over the top, not old-fashioned").
  2. Each trip (optional detail): by default the goal is "good clothes I can wear for many occasions." If there's a specific need, say so, e.g. "semi-formal for conferences, dates, office parties: shirts, a pant, shoes."
  3. Item by item: take a photo and get the verdict and a plain explanation, with visuals for anything unfamiliar.
  4. During the trip: it remembers what you bought, so later verdicts take it into account ("this pant works with the shirt you bought earlier").

Who it's for

Anyone who doesn't think about clothes, starting with you. Nothing is hard-coded to one person: each user gives it their own context.

What they do today is wander for hours, then leave with nothing or buy something "just for the sake of buying." Generic AI was too vague and full of jargon.

"If it can work for me, why wouldn't it work for others if they have the same problem as mine?"

What "working" looks like

With a profile set up, you photograph an item on your phone and get a verdict you understand and trust.
The "oh, that's cool" beat: "This would work, but in navy instead," with a swatch showing what navy looks like. Then a later item's verdict mentions the shirt you bought earlier.

Boundary

Now In the proof of concept
  • Works for any user: each person sets up their own profile, so a friend can get verdicts about their body and style
  • Usable on a phone in the store: take a photo, read the verdict
  • One-time profile: faceless body photos, height, style preferences
  • A flexible goal for each trip: defaults to "versatile clothes for many occasions," with an optional specific occasion or item list
  • Photo → skip / close-with-change / buy verdict and a plain explanation
  • Visual explanation of jargon (colors, cuts, collar styles)
  • Memory of what you bought on the current trip only
Later Worth doing, not now
  • Knowing your wardrobe: needs a full catalog up front and a second matching system before the core is proven.
  • Privacy and public sign-up: accounts, private storage of body photos, hosting for strangers.
  • Distribution: a hosted web app vs. open source people run themselves (which helps with privacy).
Cut Dropped entirely

Nothing cut outright. Everything outside the boundary is under Later.
