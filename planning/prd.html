Shopping Assistant — what it does

The product, screen by screen: what you see, what you can do, and how you'd know it's working. Still the smallest version that proves the idea. The source of truth is planning/prd.md; this page is a friendlier way to read it.

How the scope grew into this

Scope said: "the helper remembers what you've bought."
Now: a Bought items list on Home with a picture, name and a one-line note. You can delete a mistake (with a confirmation), and it's forgotten.

Scope said: "close: look for it in X color."
Now: on a Close verdict you can choose Keep looking. It waits under Still looking for until you tap Found it in another store.

Scope said: "set up a profile once."
Now: six short steps (name, body photos, skin photo, height, style cards) and a summary where you redo anything that's wrong.

Scope said: "plain, direct, like a friend."
Now: the Warm & friendly look (cream, terracotta, rounded letters, pill buttons), and Home greets you by name.

The verdict

  • One of three, big at the top: Skip it, Close, Buy it.
  • A few plain sentences: shape, color against your skin, your style, what you've bought this trip.
  • Unfamiliar words come with a swatch or small picture, never a generated image.
  • Keep looking appears only on Close. Nothing is kept unless you choose it.
  • A blurry, dark, or non-clothing photo gets "Bad photo, try again."
  • Every verdict shows exactly one outcome at the top.
  • Every color or style term has a visual beside it.
  • Close always says what to look for instead.

Every behavior, with how you'd know it works

Building a profile · name, photos, height, style

One screen per step: name, faceless body photos, a skin photo, height, style. Photos do as much as possible; you only type what a photo can't show. Style is picture cards with captions plus a free-text box.

  • Finishing every step leads to the summary.
  • Tapped style cards look selected; the free text is kept.
Profile summary

Shows the body, skin tone, and style the helper worked out. If something's wrong, you redo that step.

  • The summary matches the photos and answers given.
  • Redoing a step changes the summary.
Shopping trip · start, remember, end

Go shopping starts it (with the optional goal); Done shopping ends and clears it. Bought items count in every later verdict, and the trip survives closing the app.

  • After buying a shirt, a later pant verdict says whether they go together.
  • Reopening mid-trip shows everything intact.
  • After Done shopping, nothing carries over.
The verdict

See the Verdict screen above. Skip / Close / Buy, plain reasons, visuals for unfamiliar terms, and the bad-photo message.

  • A photo of something that isn't clothing gets "Bad photo, try again", not a verdict.
Bought items

Mark bought adds it with a picture, name, and note. Delete asks first and makes it forgotten.

Still looking for new

For Close items you really like but can't get here. You choose Keep looking; it shows what to look for. Found it moves it into Bought items (showing the variation, e.g. "Oxford shirt, navy"). Gone at Done shopping.

  • Only Close verdicts offer Keep looking.
  • Found it removes it here and adds it to Bought items.

States and edges

What you see when…
  • First open, no profile: Welcome.
  • No trip: "Hi [name]" + Go shopping.
  • Trip, nothing bought yet: no empty sections, just the buttons.
  • Bad photo: "Bad photo, try again"; nothing added.
What stays and what goes
  • Stays: your profile; the current trip, across closing the app.
  • Goes at Done shopping: bought and kept items.
  • Goes right away: checked items you didn't buy or keep; a bought item you remove.

In now, and not yet
